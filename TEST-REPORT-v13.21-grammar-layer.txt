3. Scope note

No Cambridge question text or answer was copied/generated into the app. Where a verified Cambridge English activity was available, the app links to the official activity. Other topics link to Cambridge Grammar Today.

4. Known limitation
The 34-topic map does not yet create new local interactive exercises for every grammar topic. That would require a separately verified question bank or permission-safe original exercises. The map therefore provides precise study routing without inventing source/task mappings.
